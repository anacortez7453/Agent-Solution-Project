RETURN & SHIPPING POLICY -- FICTIONAL DEMO STORE

RETURNS
Items may be returned within 30 days of the delivery date for a full
refund, provided the item is unused and in its original packaging.
Opened electronics (earbuds, chargers, speakers) can only be returned if
they are defective -- once the seal on the box is broken for a
non-defective reason, the item is final sale.

To start a return, the order must have a status of "delivered." Orders
still marked "processing," "shipped," or "delayed" are not yet eligible
for return since they haven't arrived yet.

REFUNDS
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business
days after the returned item is received and inspected. Shipping fees
are non-refundable unless the return is due to a defective or incorrect
item.

CANCELLATIONS
Orders can be cancelled free of charge any time before they ship. Once an
order's status changes to "shipped," it can no longer be cancelled -- the
customer must wait for delivery and then initiate a return instead.

SHIPPING DELAYS
Orders marked "delayed" are experiencing a shipping carrier delay beyond
the store's control. Customers should be told the expected delivery
window is an additional 3-5 business days, and offered a $10 store credit
as an apology for any delay longer than 5 business days past the original
estimate.

DAMAGED OR MISSING ITEMS
If a customer reports an item arrived damaged or a package never arrived
despite showing "delivered," a replacement should be shipped immediately
at no cost, without requiring the customer to return the original item
first.

WARRANTY
All electronics carry a 1-year manufacturer warranty covering defects in
materials or workmanship. The warranty does not cover accidental damage,
water damage, or normal wear and tear.
