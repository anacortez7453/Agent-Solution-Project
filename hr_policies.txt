EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK EXCERPTS -- FICTIONAL DEMO COMPANY

PAID TIME OFF (PTO)
Full-time employees accrue 15 PTO days per year, credited monthly at a
rate of 1.25 days/month. Part-time employees accrue PTO on a prorated
basis according to their scheduled hours. Contractors are not eligible
for PTO through this program. PTO requests should be submitted at least
5 business days in advance and require manager approval. Unused PTO
carries over up to a maximum of 5 days into the next calendar year;
anything beyond that is forfeited.

REMOTE WORK STIPEND
Employees who work remotely at least 3 days per week are eligible for a
$50/month home internet stipend, submitted through the expense portal by
the 5th of each month. This stipend is not available to contractors.

LEAVE OF ABSENCE
Employees on an approved leave of absence continue to accrue PTO at a
reduced rate (50%) during the first 90 days of leave, and do not accrue
further PTO after that point until they return to active status.
Employees returning from leave should contact HR to reactivate their
PTO accrual.

PARENTAL LEAVE
Parental leave is 12 weeks paid for the primary caregiver and 4 weeks
paid for the secondary caregiver, available to full-time employees after
90 days of employment. Part-time employees are eligible for a prorated
version based on their average weekly hours.

EQUIPMENT REFRESH
The standard laptop refresh cycle is every 3 years for full-time
employees. Employees can request an early refresh through IT if their
device is malfunctioning, but early refresh requests for reasons other
than malfunction require manager sign-off.

ONBOARDING
New full-time and part-time hires complete a 2-week onboarding program
covering company tools, security training, and a benefits overview
session with HR. Contractors complete an abbreviated 2-day onboarding
covering tools and security only.

OFFBOARDING / TERMINATION
Employees marked as "terminated" in the HR system retain access to
company systems for a 24-hour grace period to complete offboarding tasks,
after which all system access is revoked. Any unused PTO balance for
full-time or part-time employees is paid out in the final paycheck,
subject to state law; contractors do not receive PTO payout since they do
not accrue PTO.
